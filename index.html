01 / About the project

An excuse game for people who take nonsense seriously.

There is no useful skill here. That is exactly the point.

The Excuse Game turns everyday awkward situations into competitive storytelling. Each round starts with a random AI-generated scenario. Both players then compete to produce an excuse that is longer, stranger, and more elaborate than the other.

The AI keeps the situations unpredictable while the scoring system rewards creativity, unnecessary detail, and the sheer commitment to a terrible lie.
